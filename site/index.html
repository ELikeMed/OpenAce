How Ace Closes Deals

Your full sales pipeline on autopilot

Ace doesn't just organize — it finds leads, reaches out, follows up, and closes. Like having a top-performing sales teammate who never stops working.

🔍

Finds Your Leads

Tell Ace your target market and it searches the web, finds real businesses with real contact info, and adds them to your pipeline. No manual prospecting.

📧

Contacts & Follows Up

Ace drafts personalized emails, sends them, and follows up on schedule — exactly how a top salesperson would. No templates, no copy-paste, no forgotten leads.

📊

Manages Your Pipeline

Every lead tracked from first contact to closed deal. Ace moves stages, flags stale prospects, and tells you exactly who needs attention.

🔄

Runs While You Sleep

Schedule routines — morning briefings, pipeline grooming, lead follow-ups — that run automatically. Wake up to new leads contacted and deals moving forward.

💻

Builds What You Need

Need a landing page for a campaign? A contact form? A client portal? Tell Ace and it builds it — full code, live preview, deployed in minutes.

📱

Drives Inbound Traffic

Draft, schedule, and post across Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. Create content that brings leads to you while Ace handles outbound.
